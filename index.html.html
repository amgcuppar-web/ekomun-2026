About the conference

EkoMUN is a student-run Model UN held in Wrocław and organised by students of the International School of EKOLA. 2026 is its second year.

Wrocław already has a lively MUN scene, and the team wants to raise the standard of debate even further, with committees for both first-timers and experienced delegates. The theme nods to the city's past on the ancient amber trade route, a place where people met, traded and exchanged ideas.
