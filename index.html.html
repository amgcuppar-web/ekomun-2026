About EkoMUN

EkoMUN is a student-run Model UN held in Wrocław, organised by students of the International School of EKOLA. 2026 is its second edition.

The theme

Wrocław sat on the old amber trade route, a place where people came to trade and talk. The organisers want the city to be known for youth dialogue in the same way, with committees for beginners and experienced delegates alike.

Going with Ghamanga (GMC)? We are taking a delegation to EkoMUN and help students with applications and preparation. Our support fee is separate from the conference fee.
