Join the GMC delegation

Go to EkoMUN as part of a group from Ghamanga, with help from your application through to the conference weekend.

Please read this first. GMC is an independent delegation. EkoMUN 2026 is organised by students of the International School of EKOLA, and its fees, seats and decisions belong to them. You still apply to the conference on its official page. GMC's fee below is separate and is paid to GMC for our own services.

What GMC does for you

  • Application support ($10): help choosing a committee and completing your EkoMUN application.
  • Full delegation support ($19): everything above, plus group coordination and preparation before the conference.

How it works

  1. Tell GMC you are interested using the button below.
  2. Pick a GMC support option and pay the GMC fee as we instruct.
  3. Apply to EkoMUN on the official page, with our guidance.
  4. If accepted, pay the EkoMUN delegate fee (PLN 225) to the organisers.
